A custom piece begins as a conversation, and ends as a garment that could only ever be yours.

Our cutters and seamstresses work around one long table, where every piece is drawn, cut and finished by hand. We take on a small number of custom pieces each month, so each one has our full attention.

How it works

Four steps, about six weeks.

  1. 0145 minutes

    The consultation

    Tell your designer the occasion and how you want to feel. We sketch together and take your measurements.

  2. 02Weeks 1 to 2

    Fabric, chosen and cut

    Silk, crepe, lace, linen or a Kente-inspired weave. Once you approve the sketch, we cut by hand.

  3. 03Weeks 3 to 5

    The fittings

    Two fittings, sometimes three, until the piece moves the way you do.

  4. 04Week 6

    Ready for you

    Pressed and waiting at your boutique. The balance is paid when you collect.
