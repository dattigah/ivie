Collection and delivery

Collect in storeEast Legon or Spintex. Ready the same day when the piece is in stock. Complimentary.
Accra deliveryNext day by our own rider. GHS 60, complimentary for Gold and Onyx clients.
Across GhanaTwo to four days by courier, tracked by WhatsApp. From GHS 120.

We hold reserved pieces for 48 hours. Every order arrives wrapped in our tissue and box, with a note from your stylist.

Returns and exchanges

Ready-to-wear pieces can be exchanged or returned within 14 days, unworn and with their tags. Bring them to either boutique, or message us and our rider will collect in Accra.

Refunds go back to the mobile money wallet or card you paid with, within five working days. Altered ready-to-wear and custom pieces cannot be returned, but we will always refit them.

Custom order terms

  1. 1A 50% deposit holds your place in the atelier calendar and buys your fabric. The balance is due at the final fitting.
  2. 2Your designer shares a sketch and fabric swatches for approval before anything is cut.
  3. 3Two fittings are included. Further fittings and changes after approval are quoted first.
  4. 4If you cancel before cutting, we refund the deposit less the cost of fabric already bought.

Privacy

We keep your name, contact details, measurements and style notes so your stylist can serve you well. We never sell them. We never see your MoMo PIN or full card number. You choose how we contact you in your account, and you can ask us to delete your details at any time, in line with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012.

Terms

Prices are in Ghana cedis and include VAT. Colours on screen can differ slightly from the fabric, so we are happy to send a swatch photo on WhatsApp before you buy. Membership benefits are personal and are reviewed each year.
